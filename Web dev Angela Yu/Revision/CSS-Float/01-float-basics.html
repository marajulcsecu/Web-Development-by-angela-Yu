CSS Float for Text Wrapping

I am Floated LEFT
Notice how this text wraps around the blue box. Float pushes the box to the left side of its container, and the inline text is forced to flow into the remaining space on the right. This is exactly what floats were invented for: wrapping text around images. If there is enough text, it will eventually wrap completely under the floated element, creating a newspaper-like layout.

I am Floated RIGHT
Now this box is floated to the right. The text naturally flows around it on the left side. Notice the margins applied to the boxes to prevent the text from pressing right up against the edges.
